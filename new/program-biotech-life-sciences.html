BIOTECHNOLOGY & LIFESCIENCES

Internships for students of 9th – 12th grades

The Biotechnology/Biosciences/Life Sciences program is structured for students in Grades 9–12 planning STEM undergraduate applications in biomedical and life science disciplines. This internship is delivered in association with Advanced Genomics Institute & Laboratory Medicine Pvt. Ltd. (AGILE) and its Labassure diagnostic division, organizations operating in genomics research, molecular diagnostics, and genetic medicine.

Students are exposed to laboratory research environments, clinical diagnostics workflows, cytogenetics, and molecular biology systems through facility immersion and guided project environments. Students work on live or closely modelled laboratory and research processes related to genetic analysis, disease diagnostics, molecular testing, and biomedical data interpretation.

Designed for students planning undergraduate applications in biotechnology, genetics, molecular biology, biomedical sciences, medicine, or life sciences research pathways.

What do the interns get?

Interns receive structured exposure to clinical, research, and diagnostic biotechnology ecosystems through guided training and laboratory participation.

  • Exposure across biomedical research, diagnostics, and laboratory operations
  • Participation in genetics and molecular biology workflows
  • Interaction with researchers and diagnostic specialists
  • Individual and group project work
  • Industry and academic exposure (India & international perspective)
  • Certification, project documentation & letter of recommendation

Interns also shadow laboratory professionals, participate in research discussions, and engage with academic experts and biotechnology practitioners.

Topics Of Study

  • Human biology
  • Basics of human genetics including chromosomal and metabolic disorders
  • Cell biology and immunology
  • Genetic inheritance and pedigree charting
  • Chromosomal and metabolic genetic disorders
  • Genetic counselling frameworks
  • Cancer biology
  • Molecular biology

Hands-On Demonstrations / Projects

Students observe and participate in nucleic acid extraction processes used in diagnostic and research environments, understanding sample preparation, purification, and analysis workflows.

Exposure to Polymerase Chain Reaction amplification techniques used in genetic testing, pathogen detection, and molecular diagnostics, including sample processing and result interpretation.

Understanding cell growth environments, media preparation, incubation conditions, and contamination control within research laboratory settings.

Observing enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay workflows used for antigen-antibody detection in clinical diagnostics and biomedical research.

Exposure to metabolomic testing systems used for detection of inborn errors of metabolism and biochemical profiling.

Understanding contamination prevention, sterile handling, and safety cabinet workflows in regulated laboratory environments.

Partner Profile

Advanced Genomics Institute & Laboratory Medicine (AGILE) is a biotechnology research organization focused on genomic diagnostics, cytogenetics, and molecular medicine applications.

Research at AGILE is directed toward improving understanding of genetic disorders, metabolic diseases, and biological mechanisms, with translational focus from laboratory research to clinical deployment.

Labassure operates advanced diagnostic laboratories offering services in cytogenetics, molecular biology, immunoassays, haematology, and biochemical diagnostics.

MILS Labassure International, its Mumbai facility, specializes in newborn screening and metabolic disorder detection using Gas Chromatography / Mass Spectrometry platforms.
