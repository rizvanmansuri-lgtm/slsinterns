Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how the programs work.

Students in Grades 9–12 who are planning undergraduate applications in STEM, business, media and related fields.

Each program runs for 10 working days, divided into four phases: initial training and briefing (20%), project work (30%), research and analysis (30%), and report writing and presentation (20%).

Programs are in-person and online. Training and the final presentation happen at the facility, project work takes place at partner industry locations, and research is carried out remotely.

An internship certificate and project report, a letter of recommendation, and direction towards research papers in student journals.

Yes. The Standard Research Paper converts existing project work into a formal paper in about 5 working days. The Advanced Research Paper is a full research engagement of 15–20 working days across a 90-day cycle, with peer review before submission.

Use the Enquire Now button or the contact page. The form has a field for travel and accommodation support, and our team will respond with the details.

We address a gap many high-school students face while preparing for competitive university applications: they have academic interest, but no structured proof of it.

Today's undergraduate applications ask for more than grades. Universities look for internships, research exposure, project work and professional recommendations that validate a student's interest in a chosen field. Finding relevant opportunities, credible mentors and structured pathways is hard, and our programs are built to close that gap and strengthen every student's profile.
