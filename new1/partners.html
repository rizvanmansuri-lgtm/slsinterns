Our Partners

SLS Interns collaborates with a network of industry organizations, research institutions, consulting firms, and deep-technology companies to deliver structured internship and research experiences for students in Grades 9–12.

Each partner contributes to program design, domain training, mentorship, facility exposure, and project evaluation. This ensures students gain credible, application-relevant experience aligned with undergraduate aspirations.

Blockchain & Artificial Intelligence

TimeChain Labs Pvt. Ltd.

TimeChain Labs is a deep-technology infrastructure company focused on building regulated blockchain systems for financial institutions, governments, and digital public infrastructure.

The organization works across tokenization frameworks, digital asset systems, compliance infrastructure, custody platforms, and decentralized identity architecture. Their work includes blockchain deployments for banking, fintech, and sovereign digital systems, alongside AI-powered agents for financial monitoring and compliance workflows.

Biotechnology & Lifesciences

Advanced Genomics Institute & Laboratory Medicine (AGILE) — Labassure Division

Advanced Genomics Institute & Laboratory Medicine (AGILE) is a research-driven biotechnology organization focused on advancing genetic and molecular diagnostics to improve clinical outcomes.

Its work spans cytogenetics, molecular biology, biochemical genetics, and genomic research, with applications in metabolic disorders, prenatal screening, cancer biology, and inherited genetic conditions.

LABASSURE, the diagnostic arm of AGILE, operates advanced laboratory facilities offering services in molecular diagnostics, immunoassays, ELISA testing, haematology, and cytogenetics. The organization also operates MILS Labassure International, a specialized newborn screening facility equipped with Gas Chromatography/Mass Spectrometry systems for metabolic disorder detection.

Interdisciplinary Engineering

Crimson Energy Experts Pvt. Ltd.

Crimson Energy Experts is a consulting and engineering services company specializing in nuclear energy systems, marine propulsion technologies, and high-complexity infrastructure projects.

The organization’s expertise spans design and engineering of nuclear plant systems, safety analysis, regulatory documentation, commissioning support, and reactor infrastructure development. Its work also includes services related to Small Modular Reactors (SMRs), radiation safety, fuel handling systems, and climate-aligned nuclear energy solutions.

Crimson has executed projects and consulting assignments across defense, maritime, and energy sectors, with clients including government and large engineering organizations.

Finance & Economics

LeadStrategus Pvt. Ltd.

LeadStrategus is a global B2B growth consulting firm specializing in go-to-market strategy, econometric modelling, sales analytics, and revenue expansion for technology companies and startups.

The firm works across three primary service areas: market entry advisory, analytics-driven sales execution, and outsourced demand generation programs. Its consulting work spans sectors including AI, SaaS, EV infrastructure, fintech, and cloud platforms.

Students are exposed to venture capital ecosystems, investment evaluation frameworks, financial modelling, market research methodologies, and consulting case development based on real client engagements.

Aeronautical & Aerospace

Manastu Space Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Manastu Space Technologies is a propulsion engineering company focused on developing green propulsion systems for satellites and launch vehicles.

The organization works across thruster design, propellant chemistry, propulsion integration, spacecraft mobility, and sustainable space technologies. Its facilities include propulsion testing infrastructure, clean rooms, integration bays, additive manufacturing systems, and simulation environments.

Manastu is engaged in propulsion development projects for satellite platforms and collaborates with defense and international launch ecosystem stakeholders.

Electronics & Instrumentation Product Design

System Level Solutions (India) Pvt. Ltd.

System Level Solutions is a global electronics product engineering and manufacturing company providing end-to-end solutions across hardware, firmware, semiconductor systems, and embedded technologies.

The organization specializes in product design, prototyping, validation, and mass manufacturing across industries. Its facilities include PCB assembly lines, semiconductor design environments, RF and microwave testing labs, environmental test chambers, and advanced prototyping systems. With hundreds of engineers and international operating bases, the company delivers turnkey solutions across IoT systems, EV infrastructure, wireless technologies, and industrial electronics.

Psychology

Ascendus

SLS’s psychology programs, delivered in partnership with Ascendus, introduce students to behavioral science frameworks, applied psychology, and research-oriented exploration within mental health and human behavior domains. Programs include structured academic exposure, observational learning, and guided project work aligned to psychology pathways.

Marketing Communications

Kintel

Kintel serves as SLS’s partner for marketing communications exposure, offering students insight into branding, messaging strategy, outreach planning, and communication ecosystems. Students are introduced to how marketing functions operate across industries, including storytelling, positioning, and campaign development.

We address a gap many high-school students face while preparing for competitive university applications: they have academic interest, but no structured proof of it.

Today's undergraduate applications ask for more than grades. Universities look for internships, research exposure, project work and professional recommendations that validate a student's interest in a chosen field. Finding relevant opportunities, credible mentors and structured pathways is hard, and our programs are built to close that gap and strengthen every student's profile.
