Industrial Training

Choose from a wide range of structured training created by industry professionals. Whether you are a beginner or looking to upgrade your expertise, our training are designed to deliver practical knowledge, real-world application, and measurable results.

Aeronautical & Aerospace

The Space Technology program is structured for students in Grades 9–12 planning STEM undergraduate applications in aerospace and space engineering disciplines. This internship is delivered in association with Manastu Space Technologies, an organization working on propulsion systems and satellite mobility technologies.

Biotechnology & Lifesciences

The Biotechnology/Biosciences/Life Sciences program is structured for students in Grades 9–12 planning STEM undergraduate applications in biomedical and life science disciplines. This internship is delivered in association with Advanced Genomics Institute & Laboratory Medicine Pvt. Ltd. (AGILE) and its Labassure diagnostic division, organizations operating in genomics research, molecular diagnostics, and genetic medicine.

Explore More

Electronics & Instrumentation Product Design

The Electronics & Instrumentation Product Design & Manufacturing program is structured for students in Grades 9–12 planning STEM undergraduate applications. This internship is delivered in association with System Level Solutions (India) Pvt. Ltd., Anand, Gujarat, a turnkey electronics product engineering and manufacturing organization.Students are exposed to electronics product engineering, embedded systems, semiconductor design environments, manufacturing workflows, and testing ecosystems through facility-based immersion and guided project environments. Students work on live or closely modelled projects related to smart infrastructure, EV systems, semiconductor prototyping, aerospace electronics, and AI-led diagnostics.Designed for students planning undergraduate applications in electronics engineering, electrical engineering, robotics, semiconductor engineering, embedded systems, or product design.
