Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by System Level Solutions (India) Pvt. Ltd., 32, D4, Phase 1, GIDC Estate, V.U. Nagar – 388 121, Gujarat, India ("SLS", "we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information we collect through the SLS student internship website and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us through the "Enquire Now" and contact forms:

  • Full name, mobile number and email address
  • Subject and the requirement you describe in your message
  • Whether you need travel and accommodation support
  • The page of our website you sent the enquiry from

We do not ask for payment details, government ID numbers or any other sensitive information through this website. Please do not include them in your message.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and share information about our internship, training and research programs
  • To plan programs, including travel and accommodation support where requested
  • To keep records of enquiries and improve our programs and website

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3A. Students under 18

Our programs are designed for students in Grades 9–12, many of whom are under 18. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to review this policy and to submit the enquiry or be involved in it. We will usually communicate with a parent or guardian before enrolling a minor in any program.

4. How your enquiry is sent

Form submissions are delivered to our team by email through EmailJS, a third-party email-sending service. Your details pass through that service only so that the email can reach us. We may also use other service providers (for example email or hosting providers) who process information on our behalf and are expected to protect it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and to run our programs, and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records, unless the law requires a longer period. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently use advertising cookies. If we add analytics tools in future, we will update this policy.

7. Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@slscorp.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
