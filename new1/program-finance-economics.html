FINANCE & ECONOMICS

Internships for students of 9th – 12th grades

The Finance & Economics program is structured for students in Grades 9–12 planning undergraduate applications in business and finance-related disciplines. This internship is delivered in association with LeadStrategus Pvt. Ltd., a boutique global growth consulting firm that uses analytics and economic models to develop sales and market expansion strategies for technology companies and startups.

Students are exposed to venture capital ecosystems, investment banking perspectives, econometric modelling frameworks, and go-to-market consulting environments through real and simulated project exposure. Students work on live or closely modelled consulting briefs related to market entry strategy, customer intelligence, investment analysis, and growth planning.

Designed for students planning undergraduate applications in finance, economics, business management, entrepreneurship, or data-driven strategy domains.

What do the interns get?

Interns receive structured exposure to finance, consulting, and market strategy ecosystems through guided training and project work.

  • Exposure to venture capital and investment banking ecosystems
  • Application of econometric models in growth strategy
  • Market research and competitive intelligence training
  • Financial modelling and valuation exposure
  • AI and analytics applications in sales strategy
  • Risk assessment and investment monitoring frameworks
  • Certification, project documentation & letter of recommendation

Interns also shadow consulting professionals across operational workflows and participate in research discussions and management reviews.

Topics Of Study

  • Go-to-market strategy development
  • Competitive intelligence and buyer persona research
  • Sales enablement through data-based storytelling
  • AI tools in B2B sales ecosystems
  • Research methodologies and econometric modelling
  • Venture capital operations and investment analysis

Hands-On Demonstrations / Projects

Students develop a 30-day go-to-market strategy for a SaaS product, identifying target verticals, defining ideal customer profiles, mapping competitors, and building messaging frameworks for market entry.

Analysing high-growth companies to identify buyer triggers, social proof indicators, customer engagement patterns, and GTM timing strategies influencing purchase decisions.

Researching India’s EV infrastructure ecosystem and evaluating how startups leverage partnerships, policy incentives, and digital distribution channels to scale adoption.

Conducting comparative evaluations of AI tools used in lead qualification, targeting automation, pipeline analytics, and sales enablement environments.

Developing executive intelligence reports combining firmographics, buyer signals, organizational pain points, and engagement strategies for leadership outreach.

Reviewing startup investment decks and preparing analytical management notes assessing business models, scalability, and investment viability.
