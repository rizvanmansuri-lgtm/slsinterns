Interdisciplinary Engineering

Internships for students of 9th – 12th grades

The Interdisciplinary Engineering program is designed for students planning undergraduate studies in mechanical, civil, energy and systems engineering. It is delivered with an engineering consulting partner working on energy systems and complex infrastructure projects.

Students are exposed to engineering design, safety analysis, documentation and commissioning practice through guided project exposure and structured training modules. They work on live or closely modelled assignments that combine several engineering disciplines.

Ideal for students aiming at engineering, energy, infrastructure, industrial or systems programs.

What do the interns get?

Interns receive structured exposure to multi-disciplinary engineering through guided training and project work.

  • Exposure to engineering design workflows
  • Understanding of safety and risk analysis
  • Participation in design review exercises
  • Interaction with consulting engineers
  • Exposure to regulatory documentation
  • Certification, project documentation and letter of recommendation

Interns also observe project reviews, take part in design discussions and join commissioning exercises aligned with industry practice.

Topics Of Study

  • Engineering design process
  • Energy systems basics
  • Safety and risk analysis
  • Regulatory documentation
  • Project commissioning
  • Systems thinking
  • Technical communication

Hands-On Demonstrations / Projects

Students review a sample design package and discuss improvements with engineers.

Interns work through a simplified risk assessment for an engineering system.

Students prepare a checklist for bringing a system into service.

Interns map how mechanical, electrical and civil elements interact in one project.
