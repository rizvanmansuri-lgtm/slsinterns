Our Partners

We collaborate with a network of industry organizations, research institutions, consulting firms and deep-technology companies to deliver structured internship and research experiences for students in Grades 9–12.

Each partner contributes to program design, domain training, mentorship, facility exposure and project evaluation, so that students gain credible, application-relevant experience aligned with their undergraduate aspirations.

Blockchain & Artificial Intelligence

Partner Company One Pvt. Ltd.

A deep-technology infrastructure company building regulated blockchain systems for financial institutions, governments and public digital infrastructure.

The team works on tokenization frameworks, digital asset systems, compliance infrastructure and decentralized identity, and hosts interns on live engineering problems.

Biotechnology & Life Sciences

Partner Company Two Institute & Laboratory

A research-driven biotechnology organization advancing genetic and molecular diagnostics to improve clinical outcomes.

Its work spans cytogenetics, molecular biology, biochemical genetics and genomic research, giving students direct laboratory exposure.

Interdisciplinary Engineering

Partner Company Three Pvt. Ltd.

A consulting and engineering services company specializing in energy systems, marine technologies and high-complexity infrastructure projects.

Its expertise covers design and engineering, safety analysis, regulatory documentation, commissioning support and plant infrastructure.

Finance & Economics

Partner Company Four Pvt. Ltd.

A global B2B growth consulting firm specializing in go-to-market strategy, econometric modelling, sales analytics and revenue expansion for technology companies.

The firm works across three service areas: market entry advisory, analytics-driven sales and growth strategy.

Aeronautical & Aerospace

Partner Company Five Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

A propulsion engineering company developing green propulsion systems for satellites and launch vehicles.

Its work covers thruster design, propellant chemistry, propulsion integration and spacecraft mobility, with test facilities students can visit.

Electronics & Instrumentation Product Design

System Level Solutions (India) Pvt. Ltd.

A global electronics product engineering and manufacturing company offering end-to-end solutions across hardware, firmware, semiconductor systems and embedded technologies.

The organization specializes in product design, prototyping, validation and mass manufacturing.

Marketing Communications

Partner Company Seven

Our partner for marketing communications exposure, offering students insight into branding, messaging strategy, outreach planning and communication ecosystems.

Students are introduced to how marketing functions operate across industries, including storytelling, positioning and campaign development.

We address a gap many high-school students face while preparing for competitive university applications: they have academic interest, but no structured proof of it.

Today's undergraduate applications ask for more than grades. Universities look for internships, research exposure, project work and professional recommendations that validate a student's interest in a chosen field. Finding relevant opportunities, credible mentors and structured pathways is hard, and our programs are built to close that gap and strengthen every student's profile.
