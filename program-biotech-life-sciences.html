Biotechnology & Life Sciences

Internships for students of 9th – 12th grades

The Biotechnology & Life Sciences program is designed for students planning undergraduate studies in biotechnology, life sciences, medicine and related fields. It is delivered with a research-driven laboratory working on genetic and molecular diagnostics.

Students are exposed to laboratory workflows, diagnostic methods and quality practices through guided project exposure and structured training modules. They work on live or closely modelled assignments covering cytogenetics, molecular biology and genomic data interpretation.

Ideal for students aiming at biotechnology, biomedical sciences, genetics, pharmacy or medical programs.

What do the interns get?

Interns receive structured exposure to laboratory science through guided training and project work.

  • Exposure to molecular and genetic diagnostics
  • Understanding of laboratory workflows and quality practice
  • Participation in sample handling and analysis exercises
  • Interaction with scientists and clinicians
  • Exposure to genomic data interpretation
  • Certification, project documentation and letter of recommendation

Interns also observe laboratory routines, take part in case discussions and join data interpretation sessions aligned with industry practice.

Topics Of Study

  • Cell and molecular biology
  • Cytogenetics
  • Biochemical genetics
  • Genomic data analysis
  • Laboratory safety and quality
  • Diagnostic workflows
  • Ethics in genetic testing

Hands-On Demonstrations / Projects

Students learn how samples are received, labelled, prepared and tracked in a diagnostic laboratory.

Interns study chromosome images and learn how structural differences are identified.

A guided session on how molecular tests are set up, run and validated.

Students review sample data sets and discuss how findings are reported.
